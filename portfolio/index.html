Featured micro-realism tattoo

Lucky Maneki-neko tattoo in Berlin

An adorable Maneki-neko that brings good luck. Look closely at how it is made: this is not just a tattoo, it is true jewelry-level work on skin.

Many people think large-scale projects are the only way to express themselves. But color micro-realism makes it possible to fit incredible detail, rich shades, and real volume into a very small design. Every strand of fur and every golden coin is worked with absolute precision.

If you are looking for a micro-realism tattoo in Berlin and need an experienced artist specializing in micro-realism tattoo work, I would be happy to welcome you. Ultra-fine lines and a rich palette create a kind of magic that sits harmoniously on the body and highlights your individuality.

Technique note

✦✦✦

If you are captivated by tattoos that look like paintings on skin, you probably understand the colossal amount of work behind them. Color realism and color micro-realism are the most complex and uncompromising styles in the modern tattoo industry, leaving no room for mistakes. Creating a realistic image on living, moving tissue is far more difficult than painting on canvas. Unlike graphic styles, there are no clear outlines here to create the structure of the design. Volume and depth are built through the cleanest color transitions, correct shading, and contrast. One mistake in tone, and the tattoo becomes a flat spot.
