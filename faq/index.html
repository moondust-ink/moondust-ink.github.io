Frequently asked questions

Answers about booking, pricing, healing and what to expect at Moondust Ink in Berlin.

Write to me by email, WhatsApp or Instagram (@a.moondust.ink) with your idea, references, preferred placement, approximate size and timing. We then arrange a private consultation at Iron City Tattoo in Kreuzberg, where we plan the design before any session is booked. Moondust Ink works by appointment only.

Yes. The first tattoo consultation is free. We talk through your idea, placement, size and how the design will move with your body, and I give you an exact quote once the project is clear.

The price depends on size, level of detail, placement on the body and the number of sessions the piece needs. Color realism and micro-realism are detailed, time-intensive styles, so I give an exact quote only after we discuss your project at the consultation.

No. If we go ahead with the tattoo, the custom design and sketch preparation are already included in the final price of your session.

Everyone has a different pain threshold, and sensitivity depends on the body area. The private atmosphere at Iron City and my experience make the session as comfortable as possible. We can always take breaks, and I guide you through the whole process.

Bring references: photos of the subject itself (a pet, a flower, a gemstone) and a few tattoos whose color or style you like. Think about placement in terms of movement, not only how it looks standing in front of a mirror, and be honest about your budget and timeline. Larger color realism pieces are often built across several sessions, so knowing this upfront makes planning easier.

Color realism translates light, shadow, texture and volume onto skin with smooth color transitions instead of heavy outlines, and works for portraits, animals, flowers and sleeves. Color micro-realism applies the same detail to very small designs. Korean style tattoos are delicate pieces with soft shades and ultra-fine lines that look like airy watercolor. All three need precise technique, because there are no thick outlines to hide behind.

It depends on size, detail and placement. Small micro-realism pieces are the quickest, while larger color realism compositions such as sleeves are often built across multiple sessions. We agree on the plan at the consultation, so you know what to expect before you commit.

I use a two-step method. On day one I apply a classic protective bandage right after the session. On day two you come back to the studio, I gently clean the skin and apply a specialized healing film that does the rest of the work. Every client also receives a complimentary aftercare kit, so there is nothing extra to buy.

I work with premium pigments, pack the color densely and plan contrast and saturation for how the piece will look once it has healed, not only on the day it is finished. As with every tattoo, proper aftercare and protecting the skin from the sun are important for long-term results, and I cannot promise how any individual skin will heal.

Yes. I use only premium tattoo materials and maintain strict hygiene and sterility at every stage of the process. Iron City is a safe space where every guest is treated with respect.
